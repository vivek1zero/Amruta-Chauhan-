[ ARTIST STATEMENT ]

ABOUT AMRUTA CHAUHAN

I believe every space has a story waiting to be expressed through art. My practice is dedicated to creating artworks that are conceived for their environment pieces that become an inseparable part of architecture rather than an addition to it.

As a sculptor and mural artist, I work across a diverse range of materials including metal, ceramic, concrete, wood, FRP, and mixed media, alongside painting and sculptural installations. Each material is selected not only for its aesthetic qualities but for its ability to enhance the architectural narrative and create a lasting visual experience.

My expertise lies in conceptual design, artistic storytelling, and site-specific art curation. Every commission begins with an in-depth understanding of the space—its purpose, scale, materials, light, and human interaction. This process enables me to translate ideas into artworks that resonate with their surroundings while reflecting the vision of the client and the identity of the project.

Murals are at the heart of my artistic journey. I see them as more than surface treatments; they are immersive visual experiences that define spaces, inspire conversation, and create lasting emotional connections. Whether integrated into residential developments, hospitality environments, corporate spaces, or public architecture, my work is designed to enrich everyday experiences through meaningful artistic expression.

My philosophy is rooted in the belief that great art should not only be admired—it should be lived with. Every project is an opportunity to create a timeless artistic landmark that elevates architecture, engages its audience, and leaves a lasting impression.

"Creating art that transforms spaces into experiences."

[ HOW WE WORK ]

Our 4-Stage Turnkey Commission Process.

A meticulous, collaborative approach ensuring artistic authenticity, structural safety, and flawless architectural harmony.

01

Spatial Dialogue & Site Study

We review architectural blueprints, spatial elevations, lighting angles, and structural considerations to establish the thematic concept and scale.

02

Material Curation & Samples

Rigorous testing of kiln firings, metal patinas, stone textures, and tactile finishes. Physical sample maquettes are shared with architects and clients.

03

Atelier Handcrafting

Sculpting, casting, metal forging, and hand-finishing within our Ahmedabad studio. We welcome client visits during critical fabrication milestones.

04

Installation & Lighting Integration

Turnkey on-site mounting with engineered concealed hardware, weather-seal coatings, and custom lighting calibration for dramatic night-time presence.
