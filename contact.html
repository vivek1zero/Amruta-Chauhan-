For international commissions, pieces are engineered in modular components for crated air or sea freight, accompanied by detailed technical installation schematics and on-site supervision.
Outdoor sculptures and murals employ robust architectural-grade materials: atmospheric weathering Corten steel, marine-grade stainless steel (316L), high-temperature fired Gujarat terracotta with hydrophobic sealants, and UV-stable mineral silicate patinas. Every exterior piece is engineered to withstand heavy monsoons, intense sun, and seasonal thermal expansion.
Timelines typically range between 4 to 12 weeks, depending on scale and complexity. Concept ideation and material sample approval take approximately 2 weeks, followed by 3 to 8 weeks of artisanal handcrafting and kiln firing, culminating in 2 to 4 days for on-site structural mounting and lighting calibration.
We actively welcome architects, interior designers, and collectors to visit our Ahmedabad studio atelier by appointment. Clients frequently visit during clay sculpting, bronze patination, and pre-installation assembly to witness the handcrafted craftsmanship behind their commission.
