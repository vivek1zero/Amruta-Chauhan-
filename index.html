EXPLORE OUR CREATIVE PROCESS

01

UNDERSTAND

Every project begins with a close understanding of the space and its architectural character. The scale, proportions, materials, surroundings and purpose of the environment are carefully considered to establish an artistic direction that feels relevant to the space rather than separate from it.

learn more
02

CONCEPT & PLANNING

The initial vision is developed into a distinctive artistic concept through sketches, forms, compositions and visual exploration. Ideas are refined to establish the right balance of expression, proportion, texture and character while keeping the identity of the space at the centre of the creative process.

learn more
03

CONCEPT TO CREATION

Once the creative direction is established, the concept moves into the making process. Materials, techniques and craftsmanship come together to transform the developed idea into a refined physical artwork, with careful attention given to detail, finish, texture and the intended visual impact.

learn more
04

INTEGRATE

The completed artwork is brought into its architectural environment and becomes part of the overall spatial experience. Its scale, placement, material presence and visual relationship with the surroundings are considered so that the artwork connects naturally with the architecture and contributes to the identity of the space.

learn more
